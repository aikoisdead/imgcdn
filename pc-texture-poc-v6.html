FOR TRIAGER - v6 Exfiltration PoC

This PoC reads the authenticated /api/users/{id} response (the one that contains the email) and sends it to an external attacker server. It does not use console.log as the impact.

Reproduce in about a minute:
  1. Open an attacker catcher URL. Easiest is webhook.site (it gives you a unique URL and shows every inbound request). Or run interactsh-client.
  2. In the script at the bottom of this file, set the single CATCHER variable to your catcher host. For webhook.site use webhook.site/YOUR-UUID. For interactsh use YOUR-ID.oast.online.
  3. Log into playcanvas.com with any account, then click the red button below.
  4. Your catcher receives a request to /pc/<base64>. Base64 decode the path and you get your own profile including your email. No console, no interaction.
Do NOT paste the URL into the address bar. Chrome encodes ' to %27 there and breaks the injection. Click the link, which is the real attack vector.
