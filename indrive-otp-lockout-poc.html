inDrive OTP Verify: Lockout Absence PoC

What this proves: The OTP verify endpoint at cas-cf.euce1.indriverapp.com has no per-session attempt lockout. After N wrong guesses, a secure implementation would invalidate the session. inDrive does not. The 4-digit OTP (9,000 codes) is brute-forceable.

This PoC does NOT complete the brute force. It sends 15 wrong codes and shows that all 15 are accepted without lockout, counter, or session invalidation.
